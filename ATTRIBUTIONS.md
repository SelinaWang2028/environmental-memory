so the repository can be re-used honestly. **An image is not open just because
it appears in the project.** Items marked **TO REPLACE** came from web image
search and have no confirmed licence — they must be replaced with the project's
own photographs or openly licensed images before a fully open release.

## How to resolve the "TO REPLACE" items

1. Use the project's own photographs where they exist.
2. Otherwise use images with a known open licence — e.g. Wikimedia Commons /
   Openverse (CC BY or CC0) — and record the author and licence in this file.
3. Keep the two clearly attributed academic figures (study map, framework) and
   the Huang Hai figure with their credits.
