# Ethics, Consent and Takedown · 伦理、同意与撤回

This archive is a community record, not a dataset extracted from people. The
following commitments apply to every field site and every contribution.

## 1. Informed consent first

- No recording begins until the narrator understands the purpose, the intended
  uses, and their rights, using [`consent-template.md`](consent-template.md).
- The narrator chooses how they are identified and under what licence.
- Participation is voluntary; declining or stopping has no consequences.

## 2. The narrator keeps control

- **Attribution** — narrators may be named, or identified by role/place.
- **Anonymity** — personal names and identifiers can be withheld on request.
- **Withdrawal** — a narrator may ask to remove, replace, or de-identify their
  material at any time.

## 3. Takedown / withdrawal procedure

1. A request can be made by the narrator or a representative, through the
   project contact or a GitHub issue labelled `takedown`.
2. The material is removed or anonymised in the repository and in the live site
   at the next release, and the change is recorded in the changelog.
3. Published copies may remain in third-party archives under their own terms;
   this is explained during consent.

## 4. Sensitive material

- Material involving health, legal matters, minors, or living third parties is
  handled with extra care and withheld where consent cannot cover it.
- An interviewee's licence covers their own rights only; the project does not
  re-licence other people, places, or organisations.

## 5. Evidence and integrity

- Quotations are kept in the narrator's own language, with translation beside.
- Charted indices are researcher-coded summaries of interviews, not official
  statistics; this is stated on the relevant figures.
- Quantitative claims are traced to statutory, planning, or administrative
  records; "perceived" and "documented" change are not conflated.

## 6. Accessibility

- The site aims to meet WCAG 2.1 AA: readable contrast, keyboard navigation,
  and semantic structure.
