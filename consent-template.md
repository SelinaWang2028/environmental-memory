# Narrator Informed Consent & Licensing Form · 口述史知情同意与授权书

> Bilingual template. This is the document that makes an interview legally
> open. Adapt it to the project and use it before every recording. No interview
> is placed under an open licence without a completed form.

---

我已了解：

1. 本项目记录我对环境变化、生产生活、健康或相关经历的记忆，用于学术研究与公众教育。

## Handling notes for the interviewer

- Keep the signed form; record the licence choice in the narrator's metadata.
- If a narrator is not ready to release, mark the record `licence: internal` and
  exclude it from public data files.
- A contributor's grant of a CC licence is a grant of their own rights only;
  it cannot licence other people appearing in the recording.
